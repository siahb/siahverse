NCLEXapro 💊

Your daily dose of NCLEX practice for the Los Medanos College RN program.

Ten questions. One study habit.

NCLEXapro is designed to post ten practice questions each day in a designated Discord channel. Try each question, then tap the hidden answer and rationale to review.

The bot is being configured and is not posting yet. Daily posts will begin once hosting and a question bank are ready.

Add NCLEXapro to Discord

Server installation requires Manage Server permission. NCLEXapro needs View Channel and Send Messages.

Made for your study group

Practice together, compare reasoning, and build a daily review routine. The planned default schedule is 9 AM Pacific. Questions are supplied by the operator; the bot does not connect to or scrape paid question banks.

Study support

This is an independent student project, not an official Los Medanos College service. NCLEXapro is not affiliated with NCSBN or a question-bank publisher. Practice content is for education, not clinical advice or a guarantee of exam results.

Help and contact

Contact Siah (Siahverse) or your Discord server administrator about setup or an incorrect question. Do not send patient information, account passwords, or bot tokens.
