Privacy Policy

NCLEXapro · Effective October 1, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy describes the current NCLEXapro bot implementation and its pages on Siahverse, operated by Josiah Borja. The bot is still being set up. It will be updated before features with different data practices are introduced.

What the bot uses

The current bot uses its configured Discord channel ID, operator-supplied question text, answer choices, answers, and rationales to publish study messages. It stores posting dates, question identifiers, Discord message IDs, and the subscription announcement channel/message IDs in a local database to track delivery and avoid repeated posts and alerts.

The bot uses reactions to its subscription announcement to add or remove the NCLEXapro Alerts role. Reaction events supply a Discord user ID, which the bot uses to look up that member and update their role. It reads its own announcement and the users who reacted to restore subscriptions after a restart. Subscription membership is stored by Discord; the bot does not maintain a separate local student database. It does not read unrelated channel messages, direct messages, or individual answers, and does not track study performance. Its token is a server-side credential and should never be posted publicly.

Discord and website hosting

Question posts are sent to Discord and visible to people who can access the destination channel. Discord processes data under its Privacy Policy. Siahverse is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security under its Privacy Policy.

Website preferences

These pages use Siahverse’s shared theme setting. On siahverse.cc, an explicit theme choice is saved in the sv_theme cookie for up to one year. It stores a light or dark preference. You can remove it through your browser settings. These pages add no analytics or advertising trackers.

Retention and sharing

Posting history is kept while the bot operates to prevent repeated questions. The operator can clear this history when resetting or retiring the bot. Configuration and imported questions remain until the operator updates or removes them. The bot does not sell personal data or use student activity for advertising. Discord messages remain subject to Discord and server retention and moderation practices.

Requests and removal

Contact Josiah Borja (Siahverse) privately through your Discord server administrator for privacy or deletion requests. Do not put private information in public GitHub issues. Server administrators can remove the bot and delete its posts. Removing the bot does not automatically delete past messages or posting history.

Protect your information

Do not submit identifiable patient information, student records, passwords, or tokens. NCLEXapro has no need for these. This bot is intended for a nursing study group; members must meet Discord’s age requirements.
