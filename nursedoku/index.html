How to play

Place one RN per row, column, and care zone.

Tap to toggle an X. Drag across cells to mark several Xs in one stroke. Double-tap a cell to place or remove an RN. On a keyboard, press Enter to toggle an X or R to toggle an RN. Two RNs cannot touch, even diagonally. An RN placement that breaks a rule costs one strike; three strikes end the shift. Undo does not remove strikes.
