      Quick guide

      Using SiahDo

      Add & organize

      Unlock editing, type a task, set tags, priority, due date, or a repeat schedule, then press Enter.

      Find tasks fast

      Use search or press Ctrl/Cmd + F. Try high, p:medium, or untagged.

      Keep order

      Sort by due date, priority, tags, or newest. Use Reorder for your own custom sequence.

      Repeat automatically

      Daily and weekly tasks advance to their next occurrence when completed.

      Tip: Click the Completed heading to collapse or expand finished tasks.
