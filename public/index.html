      Quick guide

      How to use ToDo

      Add & organize

      Sign in with your Siahverse account, add a task, then attach tags, priority, a due date, or a repeat schedule.

      Focus views

      Use the overview cards to jump between all open tasks, today, overdue, and upcoming work.

      Find tasks fast

      Use search or press Ctrl/Cmd + F. Try high, p:medium, or untagged.

      Repeat automatically

      Daily and weekly tasks advance to their next occurrence when completed.
